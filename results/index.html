What we got from Flow B

We tested two login flows side by side from April 27 to August 10. Flow B asks for your email first. As of Monday it is live for everyone.

What we found along the way

Flow B's email check only looks at user accounts. It never looks at barber records. So a barber already working at someone else's shop comes back as "we don't know this email", and gets sent to create a new account.

Fixing that lookup closes most of what Flow B still misses. Small, well-scoped change.

How to read this. Every comparison above is Flow A against Flow B over the same period, April 27 to August 10 12:32pm, measured the same way for both. 7,911 devices in Flow A and 6,593 in Flow B.

What we cannot show. There is no before-the-experiment comparison for these numbers: the screens and the tracking behind them were built with the experiment, so nothing equivalent exists for the old login. Duplicate rate across the whole population also has not moved yet, because Flow A was still carrying half the traffic until Monday. That is the number to watch now.
